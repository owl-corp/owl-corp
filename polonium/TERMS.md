## Using Polonium

Polonium is provided to facilitate modmail conversations between users and server
moderators. Messages sent to or through Polonium are relayed to the moderators of
the guild you are contacting, who are responsible for how they act on that
conversation.

Our [privacy policy](./PRIVACY.md) describes what data we store as part of
providing this service.
